
# Angles

Defines how angles are represented and managed.

## Category

Definitions

## Content

Angles are expressed in degrees and represented using [Decimal](../types/decimal.md) values.

Angle origin and rotation direction depend on the active UCS settings:

- `0°` is aligned with the positive `X` axis.
- Positive angles rotate from the positive `X` axis toward the positive `Y` axis.
